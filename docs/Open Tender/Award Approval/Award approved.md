## If Award is Approved

Once all approval team members approve the award, the **Awarded** button is displayed to the highest scorer bidder.


![Award Approval](/img/open_tender/evaluation/approval/award_approved.png)

1. Award Button display.
2. Attach offer letter
3. cancel award

## Attaching Offer Letter

### Actions
-   Click **Attach Offer Letter** to upload the offer letter.

### Behavior
-   A pop-up window is displayed
-   Click the dropdown
-   Select the **Letter of Acceptance** template.
-   Click **Submit** to send the Letter of Acceptance.

![Award Approval](/img/open_tender/evaluation/approval/approval_attach_letterOfAcceptance.png)

![Award Approval](/img/open_tender/evaluation/approval/award_send_LOA.png)

## Editing Offer Letter
-   Once Attach offer letter submitted, **Edit Offer Letter** button appears

### Step 1: Click Edit Offer Letter button
- ![Award Approval](/img/open_tender/evaluation/approval/award_approval_edit_LOA.png)

Inside the edit modal:

-   **Open Editor Button** – Launch the template editor window

![Award Approval](/img/open_tender/evaluation/approval/award_approval_editOfferLetter.png)

### Step 2: Use Template Editor
![Award Approval](/img/open_tender/evaluation/approval/award_approval_templateWindow.png)
- A new popup window opens for editing the template content
- The **main tender window is locked** until the editor is closed
- Make your edits in the editor window

### Step 3: Save Changes

![Locked Main Window](/img/template/template_editor_locked_screen.png)

- Save all changes in the editor window
- Close the editor to unlock the main window
- Changes are reflected.


## Awarding Bid
-   After offer letter send is edited, click **Award Bid** Button

![Award Approval](/img/open_tender/evaluation/approval/award_approval_awardBid.png)

-   Award Bid modal appears

![Award Approval](/img/open_tender/evaluation/approval/award_approval_BidSend.png)
Inside The modal
1. Add Wining Amount
2. Add Currency
3. Add Reason For Award
4. click Submit

## Awaiting Vendor Acceptance
![Award Approval](/img/open_tender/evaluation/approval/award_approval_vendorAwating.png)

1. After clicking **Submit**, the status changes to **Awaiting Vendor** until the vendor uploads the **Offer Letter**.
2. click [**Cancel Award**](open-tender-award-cancel)

Once the vendor uploads the Offer Letter
![Award Approval](/img/open_tender/evaluation/approval/award_approval_awardTemplate.png)

1. **Download Offer Letter**
    - Users can download the uploaded Offer Letter.

2. **Select Award Template**
    - A new pop-up modal is displayed.
    - Users can select the desired Award Template from the modal.

## selecting Award Template

- The **Award Templates** button is available after the awarded vendor uploads the signed offer letter.
- Use **Award Templates** to send predefined templates to the awarded vendor.

## Award Templates
![Award Approval](/img/open_tender/evaluation/award_template/award_template_awardTemplateButton.png)

-   Click the **Award Templates** button
-   open the **Select Award Template** pop-up.


![Award Approval](/img/open_tender/evaluation/award_template/award_template_Templates.png)


1. **Select Award Template** – Choose the required award template from the dropdown list.
2. **Submit** – Click **Submit** to send the selected template to the awarded vendor.
